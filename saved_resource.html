About Jannani Platform

Jannani (PregBuddy Health) is dedicated to delivering transparent, compassionate, and location-aware maternal care for expecting mothers and newborn babies.

By integrating live GPS positioning with registered maternity centers, verified obstetricians, trimester workout guidance, and an open Mom-to-Mom experience board, Jannani ensures comfort and care at every trimester stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does the SOS Emergency Dispatch work?

A: Clicking the header SOS button acquires your active GPS location coordinates, launches an emergency alert panel, and provides a direct speed-dial link to 108 emergency services.

Q: Is the Mom-to-Mom board answered by AI?

A: No. The Mom-to-Mom Message Board is a permanent community forum created for mother-to-mother peer sharing. AI responses are strictly confined to the PregBuddy AI chatbot widget.

Terms of Service

Jannani provides informative directories and community features. In critical medical emergencies, always dial 108 or proceed immediately to the nearest emergency room.
